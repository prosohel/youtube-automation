# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## In short

We want everyone to be able to take part without harassment, whatever their experience, background or identity.

Expected:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to the project or to open source.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Not accepted:

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexual language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact details on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/prosohel), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment.
Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may block people who
repeatedly do so.
